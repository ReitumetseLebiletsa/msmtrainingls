PS

Percy Singo

FINANCE SPEAKER & PROPERTY INVESTOR

Percy Singo is a finance speaker, property investor, co-founder of Be Wise Financial Seminar, and author of the book How to Make Your Salary Work for You.

His journey into financial education began in church, where he was first introduced to teachings about managing personal finances. Inspired by these lessons, he developed a habit of consistent saving and eventually used his savings to invest in property.

Today, Percy advocates for viewing your salary as a seed rather than something to resent. He encourages people to use their income as a funding mechanism for building long-term wealth through disciplined saving and investing.

Academically, Percy holds a Master's degree in Civil Engineering from the University of the Witwatersrand. He continues to work for one of the metropolitan municipalities while pursuing his passion for financial education and property investment.

Phakiso Moleko

MOTIVATIONAL SPEAKER | COUNSELLING THERAPIST | MC

Phakiso Moleko is a motivational speaker, counselling therapist and Master of Ceremonies with experience across various platforms and audiences.

Through his counselling sessions, he has supported individuals in navigating personal challenges and finding direction. His motivational speaking engagements have included organisations such as MSM, Metropolitan Lesotho, World Vision, Harvest FM and the Lesotho Mounted Police Service.

He also brings extensive experience in conducting ceremonies, including funerals, weddings and graduation ceremonies.
