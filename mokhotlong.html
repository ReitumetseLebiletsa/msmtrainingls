Who Should Attend?

Anyone interested in personal growth, improving their mindset, developing their ideas and learning to approach life and business differently.

Change How You Think. Change How You Move.

The way we think influences the choices we make, the opportunities we recognise and how we respond when life becomes difficult.

This seminar is an opportunity to reflect, learn and explore practical ways to approach personal development, money and business with a more intentional mindset.

  • Mindset and personal growth — challenge limiting beliefs and develop a growth-oriented attitude.
  • Money and financial thinking — reflect on financial habits, choices and long-term thinking.
  • Business and opportunity — think more intentionally about ideas, decisions and action.
  • Personal responsibility — consider the practical steps you can take towards your goals.

About MSM Training Company

Supporting personal development, learning and growth through training and motivation.

Who We Are

MSM Training Company and Counselling Services, also known through its motivation and training work as Mohau and the Success Masters, focuses on personal development, motivation and learning.

Founded in 2015 by Mohau Paul Mosoeunyane, MSM aims to encourage individuals and communities to develop their potential through training, workshops and personal-development programmes.

Our Motto

We believe that personal growth begins with a willingness to learn, reflect, make choices and take action.

Our goal is to encourage people to approach their lives, relationships, goals and opportunities with greater awareness and purpose.
