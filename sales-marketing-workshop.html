Your Next Customer Won't Find You by Accident

Having a good product or service is one thing. Knowing how to communicate its value, reach the right people and turn interest into sales is another.

This workshop focuses on practical sales and marketing concepts that you can apply to your business, work or entrepreneurial journey.

Sell with Confidence

Understand selling techniques and how to communicate value to potential customers.

Market More Effectively

Explore marketing strategies that help you reach and connect with your intended audience.

Attract Customers

Learn about customer acquisition and the steps involved in moving prospects towards a purchase.

What You Will Learn

The workshop covers the following sales and marketing topics:

  • Psychology and science of selling — understand the principles behind customer decisions.
  • Effective marketing strategies — explore strategies relevant to businesses in the region.
  • 10 sales techniques — explore practical approaches to sales conversations.
  • Facebook and social media advertising — understand the role of social media ads in marketing.
  • Customer acquisition process — work through a three-step process supported by a template.
  • Sales pitch formula — explore how to present your offer clearly and persuasively.
