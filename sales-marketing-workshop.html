Learn How to Sell. Learn How to Market.

Having a good product or service is only part of the journey. People need to know about it, understand its value and have a reason to choose you.

This workshop focuses on sales and marketing ideas you can begin applying to your business and customer interactions.

  • The psychology and science of selling — understand how customers think and what influences buying decisions.
  • Regional marketing strategies — explore practical ways to promote your business and reach customers.
  • 10 sales techniques — learn approaches you can use during customer conversations.
  • Facebook and social media advertising — learn more about promoting products and services online.
  • A three-step customer acquisition process — explore a structured process supported by a practical template.
  • The sales pitch formula — communicate your offer more clearly and confidently.

About MSM Training Company

Supporting personal development, learning, motivation and business growth through training.

Who We Are

MSM Training Company and Counselling Services, also known through its motivation and training work as Mohau and the Success Masters, focuses on personal development, motivation, training and learning.

Founded in 2015 by Mohau Paul Mosoeunyane, MSM works to encourage individuals and communities to develop their potential through workshops, seminars and personal-development programmes.

Our Motto

We believe growth begins with a willingness to learn, reflect, make choices and take action.

Through our training programmes, we encourage people to develop their knowledge, improve their thinking and approach opportunities with greater purpose.
